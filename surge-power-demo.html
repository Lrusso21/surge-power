    What the company would manage

    01 · Review new requests

    See project details, service area, equipment needs, preferred timing, and customer contact information in one place.

    02 · Assign and follow up

    Assign a team member, add internal notes, and track requests through New, Contacted, Estimate scheduled, and Closed.

    03 · Confirm the next step

    A team member would contact the customer, confirm availability, and prepare an estimate. The assistant does not set pricing or book jobs.

    04 · Connect business systems

    A production version could store requests and connect email notifications, scheduling, or a CRM. Those integrations are not active in this demo.
